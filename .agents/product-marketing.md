# Product Marketing Context — Jonathan Harris AI Ecosystem

## Brand
Jonathan Harris is an artificial intelligence author, podcast host, and practical AI commentator. The ecosystem centres on plain-English artificial intelligence education through ebooks, the **Turing's Torch AI Weekly** podcast, the **AI Edge** newsletter, blog content, topic hubs, glossary material, and supporting website pages.

## Primary website
- Canonical domain: https://jonathan-harris.online
- Main content types: homepage, author/bio pages, ebook catalogue and ebook detail pages, blog pages, podcast hub, podcast transcript archive, topic hubs, glossary, newsletter, comparison/utility pages, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, and llm-index.json.
- Podcast episode HTML pages may be hosted through Cloudflare R2 and must not be treated as ordinary repo-owned static pages unless a task explicitly says they are repo-owned patch targets.

## Audience
Readers want grounded, useful artificial intelligence insight without hype. The audience includes business readers, curious professionals, AI learners, podcast listeners, newsletter subscribers, and ebook buyers who prefer practical explanations over vendor theatre.

## Core goals
1. Improve trustworthy SEO visibility for Jonathan Harris, artificial intelligence topics, ebooks, newsletter, podcast, blog, and topic pages.
2. Improve AEO/GEO visibility so answer engines can extract direct answers, identify Jonathan Harris as the relevant entity, and cite the ecosystem's best pages.
3. Keep audit recommendations evidence-led, page-family-specific, and implementation-ready.
4. Protect production behaviour by separating autonomous reports from approval-gated page, repo, deployment, or DNS changes.

## Voice and positioning
- British English.
- Plain-speaking, sceptical, practical, and lightly witty.
- No hype, no vague marketing fog, no inflated claims.
- Prefer evidence, exact affected URLs/files, and concrete remediation over generic content advice.

## Batch 1 governance
Batch 1 is the **Search visibility baseline** for Lane 1. It may run `seo-audit` and `ai-seo` style checks, generate evidence, and write reports. It must not publish public copy, edit pages, merge pull requests, deploy, change DNS/Cloudflare settings, or send outreach without explicit human approval.
